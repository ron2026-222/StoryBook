About Our Storybook

Story Book is a collection of simple and inspiring stories created for readers who love imagination, adventure, romance, and wonderful characters.

Each story brings a different world to life, filled with exciting adventures, memorable characters, challenges, friendships, and meaningful moments.

Our goal is to give readers a place where they can discover new stories, explore different worlds, and enjoy reading at their own pace.

Choose a story that interests you, turn the page, and begin your next adventure.

Featured Stories

Choose a story and discover a new world.

Rorouni Kenshin

The story begins during the 11th year of the Meiji period in Japan (1878) and follows a former assassin from the Bakumatsu, known as Hitokiri Battosai. After his work against the bakufu, Hitokiri Battosai disappears to become Himura Kenshin: a wandering swordsman who protects the people of Japan with a vow never to take another life.

Sing Yesterday For Me

Sing "Yesterday" for Me is a realistic slice-of-life romance story by Kei Toume. It follows young adults who are trying to figure out their careers, past heartbreaks, and complicated feelings for one another.

Initial D

Set in the late 1990s in Japan's Gunma Prefecture, the series follows the adventures of Takumi Fujiwara, an eighteen year old who helps his father run a tofu store by making deliveries every morning to a hotel on Akina with his father's Toyota Sprinter Trueno GT-APEX (AE86).

Bleach

Bleach follows the adventures of Ichigo Kurosaki, a high school student with the ability to see ghosts, and Rukia Kuchiki, a Shinigami. The early parts of the story focus mainly on the characters. As events unfold, the story begins to delve deeper into the world of these gods of death.

Harry Potter and the Philosopher’s Stone

A young boy discovers he is a wizard and enters Hogwarts, where he finds friendship, magic, secrets, and danger.

Black Clover

Black Clover is a Japanese fantasy story about an underdog orphan named Asta who wants to become the Wizard King in a world where magic is everything.
